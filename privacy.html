Cleaner AI: Free Up Space

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Cleaner AI: Free Up Space ("Cleaner AI", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") is provided by EEB Consultancy LLC and is designed to help users analyze, organize, clean, compress, and privately store content on their Apple devices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how information is processed, what information may be stored on your device, how advertising services operate, and how you can manage your privacy choices.

Cleaner AI uses on-device processing for its core media and organization features. The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other profile information to use its core functionality.

The free version of Cleaner AI may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Eligible Premium subscribers can use the App without advertisements. Advertising services may process certain device, usage, and advertising-related information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect and Process

Cleaner AI does not require a user account and does not intentionally collect your name, postal address, phone number, or email address to provide its core cleaning, compression, or Vault features.

The App may request permission to access information already stored on your device, including your photo library, contacts, and calendar. Access occurs only after you grant the relevant permission through iOS.

Accessing information on your device does not necessarily mean that the information is transmitted to our servers. Core media processing and organization features are designed to operate locally on your device.

Third-party advertising services integrated into the App may automatically collect or process certain information, subject to applicable privacy requirements and user choices. Depending on the configuration and permissions, this may include:

  • device information, including device type, operating system, and application information;
  • IP address and approximate geographic location derived from network information;
  • advertising identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • advertising interactions, such as impressions, clicks, and rewarded-ad completion events;
  • application interactions relevant to advertising delivery and measurement;
  • diagnostic, performance, and error information; and
  • consent signals and privacy preferences.

The information processed by advertising providers depends on the services used, device settings, applicable laws, and the permissions or choices you provide.

Cleaner AI does not provide your photo library content, contact records, calendar event content, or private Vault files to advertising networks for advertising purposes.

3. Photos and Videos

Cleaner AI may request access to your Photos library to provide features such as:

  • duplicate photo detection;
  • similar photo detection;
  • blurry photo detection;
  • screenshot identification;
  • large video identification;
  • photo and video compression;
  • storage analysis;
  • media selection and deletion; and
  • importing media into the private Vault.

Photo and video analysis is performed locally to identify content that may help you reduce storage usage or organize your media.

Cleaner AI does not automatically delete photos or videos. Deletion requires user interaction and confirmation where applicable.

When you delete an item from your Photos library, the action is performed through Apple's Photos framework and is subject to iOS recovery mechanisms, including the Recently Deleted album where applicable.

Your photos and videos are not uploaded to Google AdMob for advertising targeting.

4. Photo and Video Compression

Cleaner AI can create compressed versions of selected photos and videos. Compression is performed locally on your device according to the quality settings you select.

Cleaner AI does not intentionally upload selected photos or videos to our servers or advertising providers for compression.

Temporary files may be created locally during compression. These files are used as necessary to complete the requested operation and are intended to be removed when no longer needed.

Free users may be offered the option to watch a rewarded advertisement to access a compression operation. Rewarded advertisements are optional and may involve advertising interaction data being processed by Google AdMob.

Eligible Premium users can access applicable compression features without watching advertisements.

5. Contacts

Cleaner AI may request access to your Contacts to provide duplicate-contact detection, incomplete-contact identification, and contact merging.

Contact information is processed locally to analyze and present potential duplicates or incomplete records.

Cleaner AI does not automatically merge or delete contacts. Modifying operations require explicit user interaction.

Contact records are not shared with Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

6. Calendar

Cleaner AI may request access to your Calendar to identify old events or potential duplicate events.

Calendar information is processed locally to provide the calendar-management features available within the App.

Cleaner AI does not automatically remove calendar events. Event deletion or modification requires user action and, where applicable, confirmation.

Calendar event content is not shared with advertising providers for advertising targeting.

7. Private Vault

Cleaner AI includes a private Vault feature that allows you to store selected media within the App's private local storage.

Content imported into the Vault is stored within the App's sandboxed storage on your device.

Vault content is not transmitted to Google AdMob or used for advertising targeting.

Importing media into the Vault does not automatically delete the original item from your Photos library.

Vault content remains subject to device-level risks, including device loss, deletion of the App, device failure, or loss of local data where no backup exists.

8. Face ID and Touch ID

Where supported, Cleaner AI may allow you to use Face ID or Touch ID to protect access to the Vault.

Biometric authentication is performed by Apple's system frameworks. Cleaner AI does not receive, store, or access your facial map, fingerprint data, or biometric templates.

Biometric information is not shared with advertising providers.

9. Device and Storage Information

Cleaner AI may access storage information made available by iOS, including available storage capacity and related system-provided values.

This information is used to display storage summaries and potential storage savings.

Storage analysis results are not intentionally provided to advertising networks for advertising targeting.

10. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Cleaner AI may offer auto-renewable subscriptions, free trials, introductory offers, promotional offers, or other in-app purchases through Apple's App Store.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card number, bank account details, or Apple Account password.

The App may receive information necessary to determine purchase status, subscription status, entitlement status, trial eligibility, expiration status, and to restore previous purchases.

This information is used to provide and manage access to purchased or subscription-based features.

Subscription pricing, billing periods, available trials, introductory offers, and other purchase terms are displayed before you complete a transaction.

These terms may vary by country, region, currency, eligibility, and App Store storefront.

Eligible Premium subscribers receive an advertisement-free experience within Cleaner AI while their applicable entitlement remains active.

Your App Store purchases are also subject to Apple's applicable terms, billing practices, and privacy policies.

The free version of Cleaner AI may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, services provided by Google.

Advertisements may include banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and optional rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process device information, network information, advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and other advertising-related information to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and perform other purposes disclosed through applicable consent interfaces.

Depending on applicable law, device permissions, and your privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited.

Non-personalized advertisements may still involve limited data processing, including contextual information, approximate location, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and reporting.

Choosing not to consent to personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that all advertisements will disappear.

Google and its advertising partners operate under their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

For more information, visit Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other applicable regions, Cleaner AI uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising-related privacy choices where required.

When applicable, you may be presented with a consent message that allows you to:

  • consent to the specified data processing purposes;
  • decline consent where applicable; and
  • review and manage individual privacy preferences.

Consent is not required for every type of processing. Some processing may rely on other applicable legal bases, subject to relevant law and your available rights.

Where consent is the applicable legal basis, you may withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing performed before withdrawal.

Where available, you can review or update advertising privacy choices using the Privacy Options feature within Cleaner AI's Settings.

Advertising availability and personalization may change based on your choices, location, device settings, and applicable requirements.

13. App Tracking Transparency

Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework controls whether an application may track users across apps or websites owned by other companies, as defined by Apple.

If Cleaner AI enables advertising-related tracking that requires ATT authorization, the App will request permission through Apple's system-provided tracking authorization prompt before performing that tracking.

You may allow or deny tracking permission. Denying permission does not prevent you from using Cleaner AI's core cleaning features.

You can review or change tracking permissions through iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security and Tracking, where supported.

Cleaner AI does not treat consent provided through a GDPR message as a substitute for Apple's ATT authorization when ATT authorization is required.

Advertising identifiers and cross-app tracking will only be used where permitted by applicable laws, Apple's requirements, and the permissions you have provided.

14. Analytics, Diagnostics, and Third-Party Services

Cleaner AI is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection for its core functionality.

Google AdMob and its associated SDK components may process advertising interaction, performance, and diagnostic information for advertisement delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Cleaner AI also uses Apple frameworks and services, including Photos, Contacts, EventKit, LocalAuthentication, StoreKit, and other iOS system services where applicable.

Apple may process information related to App Store transactions, subscriptions, purchases, refunds, and your Apple Account according to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

We do not intentionally use the contents of your photos, videos, contacts, calendar events, or Vault files for third-party advertising analytics.

Third-party service providers may process and retain information under their own policies, applicable contractual obligations, and legal requirements.

15. Data Sharing and Retention

Cleaner AI does not sell your photos, videos, contacts, calendar content, Vault content, or biometric information.

We do not intentionally share such content with advertising networks or data brokers.

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in this Privacy Policy and applicable consent notices.

Advertising providers may retain information according to their privacy policies, legal obligations, and retention practices.

No system can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, passcode, Apple Account, backups, and any PIN used within Cleaner AI.
